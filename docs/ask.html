ask·200

reads ~200 sources and images, answers in one cited line — ask with words or a photo

⌕

settings — answers are extractive until you add an Anthropic key

The key stays in this browser (localStorage) and goes only to api.anthropic.com. With it, Claude writes the answer and sees your photo and the top images. Without one, the answer is the best sentences, verbatim, and a photo is read by CLIP in this tab.

the browser reads CORS-open sources (Wikipedia, Hacker News, Stack Overflow, OpenAlex, GitHub) and 5 image libraries; answer.py scrapes the whole web with Scrapling  ·  the capstone write-up  ·  CLIP·search, your gallery  ·  github
