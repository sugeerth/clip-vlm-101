Find, fast

Type and the grid re-ranks as you type — no button, no wait. The most relevant images render largest, packed edge-to-edge with no gaps. And a ranking-quality agent watches one thing: do you have to scroll? Because a good ranker puts the answer in the top 1-3 — if you're scrolling, it missed.

—
ranking quality — the answer belongs in the top 1-3
type to search
↑ you're scrolling past the top results — a good ranking shouldn't need it.

Ranking is instant and lexical by default (matches your words against each image's caption and tags); when the on-device CLIP encoder finishes loading it blends in semantic similarity and re-ranks — progressive enhancement, so results never wait on a download. The quality gauge is rankquality.js (the byte-identical twin of rankquality.py): precision@3, MRR, nDCG@5 and a scroll penalty, combined into one score. And the loop closes: 👍/👎 any tile — or click a result you had to scroll to — and the on-device learner (learn.js, pairwise RankNet, four floats in your localStorage) re-weights the ranking so next time the answer sits higher. The quality agent grades the ranker; your feedback teaches it.
